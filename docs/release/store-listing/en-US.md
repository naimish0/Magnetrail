## Full description

Every arrow has a direction. Every magnet changes the path. Clear the board by finding the right sequence.

Magnetrail is a calm arrow puzzle built around Pull and Push magnets. Choose an arrow, trace its route, and watch the magnetic field guide it across the board. After a magnetic move, polarity flips automatically—so the next route may behave very differently.

Plan ahead through handcrafted logic puzzles where order matters. Learn the rules in approachable early levels, then combine direction, polarity, and sequence reasoning in increasingly intricate challenges.

Features:
• 100+ certified campaign levels
• A new daily challenge with streak progress
• Pull and Push magnet mechanics with automatic polarity flips
• Optional hints earned through play or a rewarded ad
• Offline campaign play
• Sound, haptic, reduced-motion, and diagnostics controls
• Clear labels and scalable Android interface

Whether you enjoy an arrow puzzle, magnetic puzzle, offline puzzle game, or focused brain teaser, Magnetrail offers short sessions with room for careful reasoning. There are no accounts, leaderboards, or online requirement for the campaign.

Magnetrail contains ads. Rewarded ads are always optional. Interstitials are limited by gameplay rules and never appear during a puzzle.
