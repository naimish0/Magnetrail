# V10 human difficulty rejection

Source: `magnetrail-playtest-pet-52c263fb.csv` (68 recorded boards; SHA-256
`276fa032150287d49d7f5bac3f639d66f210f8cd6768cb085104e035a6d409bb`).

V10 difficulty calibration is rejected. All 68 boards were completed without hints, and only
26/68 ratings (38.2%) landed within one band of the assigned difficulty. Median perceived ratings
were Easy 1, Medium 1, Hard 1, Super Hard 1, Expert 1, and Master 1.5. Expert produced nine Easy and
two Medium ratings; Master produced six Easy, five Medium, and one Hard rating.

The high-band visual complaint was also structurally confirmed: all 11 tested Super Hard boards
shared one D4-normalized non-remediation core, all 11 Expert boards shared one, and all 12 Master
boards shared one. Combined, the 34 high-band boards came from only two base arrow/core templates.
V10 also left too many opening choices (medians 11/14, 12/16, and 11/18 for Super Hard, Expert, and
Master).

Therefore density and additional arrows are no longer accepted as difficulty evidence. V11 must
use new cores, bounded initial choices, deeper forced dependencies, observed mechanic families,
varied object counts, and perceptual-template limits. No 2,000-board regeneration or promotion is
authorized until the V11 human pilot is reviewed.
