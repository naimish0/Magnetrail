# Magnetrail closed-tester guide

Thank you for testing. Use Magnetrail naturally, then try the checks below. Do not click a live ad for testing; the release owner must confirm that the track uses approved test traffic.

## What to exercise

1. Install/update from the provided Play test link. Record app version, device model, Android version, and whether this was a fresh install or update.
2. Complete the tutorial and several early levels. Explain in your own words what Pull, Push, and the automatic polarity flip do.
3. Complete at least one advanced level and use restart/undo/back navigation.
4. Start and, if practical, complete the Daily challenge; relaunch and check streak/progress persistence.
5. Try a hint with enough coins and the insufficient-coin path. If offered, try an optional rewarded test ad; confirm reward is granted once only. Observe whether interstitial timing feels disruptive.
6. Turn the network off before launch and play campaign content. Restore connectivity and check that the app recovers.
7. Review sound, haptic, reduced-motion, and diagnostics controls. If you use TalkBack, Switch Access, a keyboard, or larger font/display settings, report anything unclear or unreachable.
8. Background/return, rotate or resize if supported, and restart the app around a move/ad/settings change. Confirm progress is preserved.

## Reporting a problem

Copy `FEEDBACK_TEMPLATE.md`. Include exact steps, expected/actual result, reproducibility, screenshots without personal notifications, and whether progress/coins changed. Do not send advertising IDs, consent strings, account emails, device serial numbers, or unrelated logs. Report a startup crash, lost progress, stuck consent/ad screen, unsolvable level, duplicate reward, or negative coin balance immediately as release blocking.
